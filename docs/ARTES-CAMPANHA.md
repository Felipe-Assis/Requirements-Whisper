## Prompts enviados

### Laboratório

> Use case: illustration-story. Production background for a 1920x1080 visual novel, landscape 16:9 composition. A modest Brazilian university equipment laboratory in morning light, no people. Hand-painted 2D animation background with delicate ink lines, teal-blue shadows, warm honey sunlight, consistent with a grounded illustrated office adventure. Wide eye-level view: microscope and TWO clearly separate paper reservation clipboards on a wooden bench at left-center; equipment preparation card clipped beside microscope; shared computer terminal at right with a neutral blank dark screen to receive a game UI overlay; shelves and warm tall windows behind. Calm, lived-in, appealing, not science fiction. Distinct recognizable props for point and click. Leave foreground bottom quarter relatively uncluttered for dialogue, but fill the entire canvas with the actual room, no decorative border or blank strip. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no UI overlay. Single scene, NOT diptych.

### Quarto

> Use case: illustration-story. A production 16:9 landscape background for a hand-drawn 2D visual novel. Cozy modest bedroom of a Brazilian student starting a software internship, early morning. Same fine ink lines and painted textured shading as a warm university office story, teal-blue shadows and honey sunlight. Wide eye level composition. Desk at left with a closed plain navy laptop without any logo, notebook and pen, backpack near chair, small alarm clock. Window on right with a leafy city view, simple wardrobe on far left, bed edge at bottom left. Open uncluttered central floor and bottom quarter for dialogue overlays, whole canvas is scene with no border or blank strip. No people, no text or logos, no UI. Make it feel personal and plausible, not luxurious, polished anime-inspired background art.

### Sabrina, roupa de retorno

> Edit this visual novel character reference into a next-week outfit variant for Sabrina. Preserve exact identity, skin tone, black ponytail, face proportions, friendly expression, linework, hand gesture, standing full-body pose and proportions. Change ONLY clothing: a muted teal casual overshirt open over a cream top, dark navy trousers and the same pale blue sneakers. Consistent hand-drawn 2D animation style, subtle textured shading. Single character centered, entire body and shoes visible. Remove background completely, genuine transparent alpha, no ground shadow, no text or logos. Production sprite for a visual novel.

### Notebook e pasta

> Use case: illustration-story. Production inventory icon for a hand-painted 2D visual novel about software investigation. A single dark navy closed laptop resting beside a thin amber paper evidence folder, three-quarter view, neat black ink outlines and subtle textured shading, matching warm Brazilian university adventure backgrounds. No branding, no seal, no text, no symbols resembling letters. Entire object visible, generous transparent margins, genuinely transparent background, no cast shadow, no scenery. This replaces a distorted-logo notebook asset; laptop is the dominant object, folder small.
